# Side Income Prompts

Source: @promptprism (Threads) - Claude prompts for building side income

---

## 1. Offer Creation Engine

"Build a 3-tier offer ladder for my expertise in [TOPIC] for [DEMOGRAPHIC]. Output format:
[SECTION 1] Value Ladder - Starter ($27-$297), Core ($497-$1,997), Premium ($5K-$25K+)
[SECTION 2] Deliverables - 3-5 specific items per tier with pricing justifications
[SECTION 3] Upsell sequences - 2 natural upsell paths per tier
[SECTION 4] Launch timeline - 30/60/90 day milestones
[SECTION 5] Success metrics - KPIs to track per tier"

**Variables:**
- [TOPIC]: Your expertise area
- [DEMOGRAPHIC]: Your target customer type

**Output:** 5 sections, table format for pricing tiers, numbered action items for timeline
