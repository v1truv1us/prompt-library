# Side Income Prompts

Source: @promptprism (Threads) - Claude prompts for building side income

---

## 1. Offer Creation Engine

"Build a 3-tier offer ladder for my expertise using proven revenue models. Include starter offer ($27-$297), core offer ($497-$1,997), and premium offer ($5K-$25K+) with specific deliverables, pricing justifications, and upsell sequences. Provide a complete value ladder diagram and launch timeline. Consider my niche of [TOPIC] and target audience of [DEMOGRAPHIC]."

**Variables:**
- [TOPIC]: Your expertise area
- [DEMOGRAPHIC]: Your target customer type

---

## 2. Income Opportunity Scanner

"Scan current market trends to find 3 profitable side income opportunities aligned with my skills and interests. Include market size (online education $450B by 2026), growth rate, and demand signals. Provide entry barriers, startup costs, and monetization timelines with specific action steps for each opportunity. Consider my current skills of [SKILLS] and available hours of [HOURS] weekly."

**Variables:**
- [SKILLS]: List of your marketable skills
- [HOURS]: Number of hours available per week

---

## 3. Skill Monetization Mapper

"Map my skills to revenue using the implementation expert framework. Identify which skills can package into digital products, which into services, and which into hybrid offers. Provide the three-pillar monetization structure (content authority, scalable products, optimized distribution) with specific packaging templates for each revenue stream. Consider my top skills of [SKILLS] and audience size of [AUDIENCE]."

**Variables:**
- [SKILLS]: List of your top skills
- [AUDIENCE]: Current follower/email list size

---

## 4. Pricing Strategy Framework

"Design a multi-tier pricing structure for my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] using value-based pricing models. Include starter, core, and premium tiers with specific features, payment terms, and positioning scripts. Provide competitor pricing analysis, anchor pricing tactics, and psychological pricing triggers that maximize conversion. Consider my market of [MARKET] and cost structure of [COSTS]."

**Variables:**
- [PRODUCT/SERVICE]: What you're selling
- [MARKET]: Your industry/niche
- [COSTS]: Your cost structure or budget

---

## 5. Client Acquisition Protocol

"Create a multi-channel client acquisition system using cold outreach, LinkedIn, and referral prospecting. Include timing rules (call 5-min before hour), permission-based openers, objection handlers, and 3-5 touchpoint follow-up sequences. Provide email templates, call scripts, and a weekly outreach calendar with specific metrics to track. Consider my target clients of [CLIENTS] and industry of [INDUSTRY]."

**Variables:**
- [CLIENTS]: Description of ideal client
- [INDUSTRY]: Your target industry

---

## 6. Sales Copy Generator

"Generate high-converting sales copy for my [OFFER] using proven structures: pattern interrupt, value proposition, objection handling, and single CTA. Include subject line formulas (personal, intriguing, brief), email templates (3-5 sentences), and A/B test variations with specific success metrics. Provide 5 complete email sequences for different buyer stages. Consider my target audience of [AUDIENCE] and pain points of [PAIN_POINTS]."

**Variables:**
- [OFFER]: Your product/service name
- [AUDIENCE]: Who you're selling to
- [PAIN_POINTS]: Key problems you solve

---

## 7. Passive Income Builder

"Build a passive income system using AI-powered content creation (YouTube, digital products, websites, audio). Include tool stack (Synthesia, Canva AI, ElevenLabs), content repurposing workflows, and monetization hooks (ads, affiliates, licensing). Provide a 30-day setup roadmap with daily tasks, automation scripts, and launch checklist. Consider my content comfort level of [COMFORT] and budget of [BUDGET]."

**Variables:**
- [COMFORT]: Your comfort with content (e.g., "video preferred" or "writing only")
- [BUDGET]: Initial investment amount

---

## 8. Market Demand Validator

"Validate my [IDEA] using market research tools and frameworks. Include competitor analysis, keyword demand scoring, customer sentiment extraction, and pricing window analysis. Provide a go/no-go decision framework with specific validation metrics, plus 3 alternative positioning strategies. Consider my niche of [NICHE] and target platform of [PLATFORM]."

**Variables:**
- [IDEA]: Your product/service idea
- [NICHE]: Your target market
- [PLATFORM]: Where you'll sell (e.g., Gumroad, Amazon, own website)

---

## 9. Revenue Scale System

"Design a revenue operations system to scale from [CURRENT] to [TARGET]. Include CRM consolidation, lead routing automation, predictive forecasting models, and multi-touch attribution tracking. Provide weekly review metrics, growth benchmarks, and a 90-day implementation roadmap with key milestones. Consider my current tech stack of [TOOLS] and team size of [TEAM]."

**Variables:**
- [CURRENT]: Current monthly revenue
- [TARGET]: Target monthly revenue
- [TOOLS]: Software/tools you use
- [TEAM]: Number of people (including yourself)

## 10. Profit Optimization Model

"Build a profit optimization framework using visibility rules, automation workload reduction (target 20+ hours monthly), and retention optimization (target 72%+). Include KPI dashboards, multi-channel engagement cadences, and profitability calculators with margin improvement projections. Provide monthly audit templates and optimization triggers. Consider my current margins of [MARGINS] and overhead of [OVERHEAD]."

**Variables:**
- [MARGINS]: Current profit margin percentage
- [OVERHEAD]: Monthly overhead costs
