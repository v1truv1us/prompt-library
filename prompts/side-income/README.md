## 1. Offer Creation Engine

Include starter offer ($27-$297), core offer ($497-$1,997), and premium offer ($5K-$25K+) with specific deliverables, pricing justifications, and upsell sequences. Provide a complete value ladder diagram and launch timeline. Consider my niche of [topic] and target audience of [demographic]."
