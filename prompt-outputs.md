- Volume: 20 emails/day
- Tool: Apollo.io or Instantly

**LinkedIn:**
- Connection requests: 20/day
- Engage before pitching (comment 3x first)
- DM after 3 days of engagement

**Referral Prospecting:**
- Ask every happy client for 2 referrals
- Offer $100 credit per referral
- Monthly referral reminder email

### SECTION 2: Scripts

**Permission-Based Opener:**
"Hi [Name], I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I help [target clients] solve [specific problem]. Would it be okay if I shared a quick idea that might help?"

1. Day 1: Initial outreach
2. Day 3: Value-add (share article/resource)
3. Day 7: Case study
4. Day 14: Break-up email
5. Day 30: Re-engage with new offer
